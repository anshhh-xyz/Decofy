About

Design Philosophy

Decofy Studios was founded on a simple conviction: architecture should feel inevitable, as though the building could not have taken any other shape once the site, the light, and the people who use it were properly understood.

Every commission begins the same way — long before a line is drawn, we spend time listening to the site, the client, and the constraints that will ultimately shape the most honest solution.

Approach

Research

Reading the site's context, culture and climate before proposing anything.

Collaboration

Working in step with clients, engineers and craftspeople from day one.

Craft

Material honesty and considered detail, at every scale of a project.
