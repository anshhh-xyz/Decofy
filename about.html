About Us

About Decofy Design Co.

Decofy Design Co. is Shivamogga's newest architecture and interior design studio — founded on the belief that great interiors start with great architecture. We bring design and engineering together under one team, delivering residential interiors, commercial fit-outs, architecture, and landscape design across Shivamogga.

The Team

Founded by Ar. Harsh Gupta & Er. Vinay N

Ar. Harsh Gupta — Architect, 5+ Years

Harsh is a registered architect with experience in residential and commercial architectural design. He leads Decofy's design direction, from space planning to 3D visualization, ensuring every project starts with a strong architectural foundation.

Er. Vinay N — Engineer, 10+ Years

Vinay brings 10+ years of engineering experience to Decofy, overseeing structural planning and site execution — the technical backbone that ensures every design gets built the way it was drawn.

"We started Decofy because we saw a gap — interior studios that design beautifully but don't fully understand structure, and architects who plan well but stop at the drawing. We wanted to offer both, from one team."
